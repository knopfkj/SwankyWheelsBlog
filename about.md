Welcome to the SwankyWheels Blog!

We know there are people out there with disabilities that struggle with 
things that they don't necessarily understand or have exposure to things
out in the "real world." If this something you connect with, you're in 
the right place. 

First off, a disclaimer - I am not a doctor or any medical professional. This site is intended to allow people with disablilites and others in their lives to understand that there are others out there going through the same situations you're dealing with on a daily basis.

We'll be posting blogs about navigating daily life, discussing sensitive
topics - we're looking at you bowel programs - visiting public places
and giving feedback on parking availability, sidewalk accessibility, 
ease of moving around using a wheelchair, restroom layouts, and more.

Some blog posts will be written by my caregiver to help us understand
our disabilities from their standpoiht.

We anticipate our community will include a Q&A section where you can
ask questions anonymously. Like I said earlier, I am not a doctor or
any other medical professional and always follow the recommendations
of your care team.
